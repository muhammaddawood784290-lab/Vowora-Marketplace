# Vowora Dashboard Requirements

Vendor and Planner dashboards share 80–90% of their shell, navigation, CRM, profile, packages, availability, notifications, and analytics components. Permissions and role-specific modules differ.

## Customer view

Marketplace, event brief, shortlist, proposal, booking, invoices, payment status, notifications, and account settings.

## Vendor and Planner view

Overview, requests or assigned events, CRM, profile, services, packages, rates, portfolio, availability, proposals, invoices, automation templates, analytics, team members, branding, and settings.

## Visual system

Black text, white surfaces, neutral gray borders, monochrome icons, clear spacing, and accessible contrast. No colored feature-specific themes.
