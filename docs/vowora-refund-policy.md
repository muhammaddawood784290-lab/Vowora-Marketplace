# Vowora Refund Policy Draft

Until gateway settlement is enabled, Vowora does not process event payouts or refunds. Vendors and planners handle direct customer payment obligations under their published terms.

Future gateway rules must define refund authority, cancellation windows, payout timing, failed payment handling, disputes, taxes, and records retention before launch.

## Status

- **Partially implemented** — policy draft exists.
- **Planned** — full refund and payout terms.
