# Vowora Reviews and Disputes

## Reviews

- Customers and providers give feedback.
- Reviews are scoped to an event.
- Status transitions are auditable.

## Disputes

- Dispute workflow is available to admins.
- Dispute states: open, evidence_requested, declined, resolved, closed.
- Evidence is stored in object storage.

## Status

- **Partially implemented** — policy documents exist.
- **Planned** — dispute and review workflow.
