# Vowora Compliance Requirements

## Requirements

- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Secure password hashing.
- MFA for admins.
- Least privilege.
- Object-level authorization.
- Data minimization and consent.
- Retention and deletion workflows.
- Legal review for out-of-market launches.

## Status

- **Partially implemented** — architecture and policy documents exist.
- **Planned** — compliance controls and certification.
