# Vowora Emergency Protocols

## Mandate

- Detect, contain, recover, and document incidents.
- Maintain minimal service.
- Escalate per the severity matrix.

## Severity levels

- S1: major outage or data breach.
- S2: significant degradation.
- S3: minor issue.
- S4: cosmetic or low impact.

## Workflow

1. Detect and alert.
2. Triage and assign severity.
3. Contain and mitigate.
4. Recover and verify.
5. Document and review.

## Escalation

- Page on-call engineer.
- Escalate security incidents.
- Escalate S1 to executives.
