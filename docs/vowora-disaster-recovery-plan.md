# Vowora Disaster Recovery Plan

## Objectives

- Restore service after a major failure.
- Protect data from loss.
- Verify restores in drills.

## Strategy

- Automated backups.
- Point-in-time recovery where supported.
- Restore to a staging environment before promoting.
- Verify data integrity before restoration.

## Status

- **Planned** — recovery procedures are documented, not yet tested.
