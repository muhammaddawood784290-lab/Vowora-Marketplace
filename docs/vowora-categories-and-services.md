# Vowora Categories and Services

## Categories

- Reception venues.
- Ceremony venues.
- Catering.
- Photography.
- Floral design.
- Music and entertainment.

## Services

- Services are scoped to a vendor or planner and include name, description, category, price, currency, duration, and availability.
