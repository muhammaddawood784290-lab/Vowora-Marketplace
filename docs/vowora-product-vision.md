# Vowora Product Vision

## Vision statement

Vowora makes event planning feel effortless. We connect customers with trusted vendors and planners through a clean, transparent marketplace where discovery, comparison, and coordination happen in one place. The platform earns trust by keeping business rules honest, by never pretending to control direct payments, and by letting customers close events with confidence.

## Mission

Give customers a trusted place to find the right team, and give vendors and planners a professional tool to grow their business. Build the marketplace our users actually want to use: fast, calm, and built to last.

## Values

- **Trust** — verified profiles, clear terms, and honest financial labeling.
- **Clarity** — black-and-white visual system, plain language, obvious next steps.
- **Ownership** — the platform controls its own data, storage, automation, and configuration.
- **Patience** — MVP is scoped. We do not build payment custody, live chat, or AI intelligence until they are proven needed.
- **Craft** — no feature-specific themes, no premature microservices, no hardcoded plan limits.

## Target users

- **Customers** planning weddings, engagements, birthdays, corporate events, and social gatherings across the US, EU, and Gulf markets.
- **Vendors** offering photography, florals, venues, catering, music, rentals, and specialist services.
- **Planners** coordinating event briefs, shortlists, vendor coordination, proposals, and bookings.
- **Admins** configuring plans, verifying businesses, reviewing assignments, disputes, subscriptions, and audit events.

## Success definition

- Customers discover, shortlist, and approve at least one event with a single provider in under 30 minutes.
- Vendors and planners can manage their business from a shared dashboard with 80–90% code reuse.
- The admin panel covers configuration, verification, assignments, disputes, subscriptions, and audit review.
- The platform never claims payment verification; invoices and evidence are clearly labeled.

## Future direction

- Managed workflows, CRM tools, analytics, and automation templates.
- Executive dashboard, portfolio, rates, availability, team members, branding, and automation pages for vendors and planners.
- Internationalization for US, EU, Gulf, and other markets.
- Controlled migration from a modular monolith to a service-oriented architecture when size or team boundaries justify it.
