# Vowora Tax and Financial Compliance

## Scope

- Invoices and financial records are informational in MVP.
- The platform does not hold, route, settle, or verify event funds in MVP.
- Future gateway rules must define tax treatment, failure handling, and records retention.

## Requirements

- Currency and tax rules configurable by country.
- Integer minor units for money.
- Auditable corrections for financial records.
- Legal review before publication.

## Status

- **Planned** — tax and financial compliance to be finalized with legal.
